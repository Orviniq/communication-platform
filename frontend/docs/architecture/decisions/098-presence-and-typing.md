## Decision (Position)

Implemented end-to-end encrypted typing indicators and online/last-seen status over volatile `signal` frames (`CPPSV001`) shared with voice signalling through one single-reader router, with a durable probe ledger capping dead skipped keys:

1. **D1 — Single `SignalRouter` for all inbound `signal` frames ([signal_router.dart](../../../lib/core/application/signal_router.dart)):**
   - Moved `SignalRouter` and `SignalFramePacer` into `lib/core/application/` (and `DeterministicCbor` into `lib/core/protocol/`). `SignalRouter` is the sole subscriber to `GatewayVoiceSignalSocket.inboundBlobs`, attached/detached by the delivery session.
   - Buffers up to 256 blobs in memory (dropping oldest past 256, matching server queue depth), drains in arrival order, and serializes opening and every channel's sealing under `SignalRouter.exclusively` (retrying once on durable-path compare-and-set loss).
   - Routes by 8-byte plaintext prefix: `CPVSV001` → voice (`VoiceSignalTransport`, preserving its 32/24 pacer, 32-frame join budget, and existing tests); `CPPSV001` → presence; unknown prefixes leave the ratchet step uncommitted so replayed durable envelopes still open on the durable path, while the durable path rejects `CPPSV001`/`CPVSV001` before decoding.
   - Any frame with a recognized magic commits its ratchet step *before* the channel checks version/kind/body/sender/switch (preventing dead skipped keys on the receiver). Every committed frame notifies the presence probe ledger of the authenticated sender device.
2. **D2 — `CPPSV001` encrypted payload format ([presence_signal_codec.dart](../../../lib/features/signals/domain/presence_signal_codec.dart)):**
   - Format: `"CPPSV001" || version:u8 (= 1) || kind:u8 || deterministic-CBOR body` (RFC 8949 §4.2.1 small uint keys; keys 0–3 header `sender_user_id`, `sender_device_id`, `instance_id`, monotonic `counter`; 4–7 reserved; 8+ body), always padded into the **1024-byte** signal bucket (max active `typing` payload 112 B, bucket capacity 942 B; no initial headers per [ADR-077](077-voice-mesh.md) decision B).
   - Four kinds: `1 = watch` (`conversation_id`, `lease_ms` 1..60,000 sent as 40,000), `2 = here` (`conversation_id`, `typing`), `3 = typing` (`conversation_id`, `active`, optional `ttl_ms` 1..15,000), `4 = gone` (`reason`: `1` left foreground, `2` switch off).
   - Receiver accepts frames only for held direct chats with that sender or active shared groups where both accounts are active (or `gone` from a watched/leased/online device), never from own devices, Saved Messages, unverified, or blocked peers. Legacy `ApplicationEventKind.typingSet` (kind 8) remains unused and refused durably.
3. **D3 — Typing policy (later amended by [ADR-099](099-typing-sessions.md)):**
   - Sends `typing(active: true, ttl_ms: 15000)` on first non-empty edit (or edit after stop), repeats every 10 s while edited in the last 5 s, stops 5 s after last edit and on send/clear/leave/background, paced at most once per 5 s per conversation (later narrowed by [ADR-099](099-typing-sessions.md) so sends emit no frame and starts bypass the 5 s pace).
   - Receiver expires typing after 15 s, or immediately on stop, `gone`, or when an incoming message from that sender is projected in the open chat. Sends only to devices holding a watch lease (< 40 s) in groups up to **32** members.
4. **D4 — Status policy (later amended by [ADR-100](100-socket-presence.md)):**
   - Foreground open chat with switch on watches the conversation: all live devices of a direct peer (≤ 10), or in groups ≤ 32 members up to 2 devices per other active member (≤ 62, sorted by newest last seen / device ID).
   - Renews every 25 s to targets heard within 40 s (10 s answer window before dropping back to probing). Foreground devices with switch on reply `here` immediately and hold a 40 s lease; background devices never answer `here` even when sustained delivery holds the socket open.
   - Online lasts 40 s from any accepted `watch`/`here`/`typing` frame or until `gone`.
5. **D5 — Foreground departure `gone` (later amended by [ADR-101](101-presence-goodbye.md)):**
   - On leaving foreground (or turning switch off), sends pending typing stops and `gone` to live leases in either direction; `SyncLifecycleSupervisor._enterBackground` waited up to 500 ms before closing the socket (extended to 1,500 ms and decoupled from queue/network waits in [ADR-101](101-presence-goodbye.md)).
6. **D6 — Probe ledger and skipped-key bound ([presence_probe_ledger.dart](../../../lib/features/signals/application/presence_probe_ledger.dart)):**
   - **P1 (Silence):** At most **4** probes to a device since the last frame committed from it on any channel (presence, voice, or durable pairwise envelope).
   - **P2 (Bucket):** Token bucket capacity **4**, refilling **1 token / 6 hours**, min spacing **1 minute** (≤ 8 probes/24 h/device).
   - **L1 (Live cap):** At most **8** live frames (`watch` renewal, `here`, `typing`, `gone`) between two committed frames from that device (in-memory).
   - Any committed frame from that device (including durable envelopes reported by `CommittedEnvelopeSenderPort`, without treating durable envelopes as making a device live) resets P1 and L1, never P2.
7. **D7 — Pacing and peer reads (peer cache later amended by [ADR-101](101-presence-goodbye.md)):**
   - Dedicated `SignalFramePacer` (capacity 16, refill 12/s) alongside voice's 32/24/s and 10/s acks (worst second 56 + 28 + 10 = 94 ≤ 100 socket limit).
   - `CachedPresencePeers` ([cached_presence_peers.dart](../../../lib/features/signals/infrastructure/cached_presence_peers.dart)) batches up to 64 users via `POST /api/v1/peers` ([ADR-080](080-batched-peers.md)) with a 10 s TTL (invalidated immediately on local trust/block changes); later extended to 60 s stale-while-revalidate in [ADR-101](101-presence-goodbye.md).
8. **D8 — Durable ledger and local last seen (schema 26, `presence_devices`):**
   - Schema 26 adds `presence_devices` in the encrypted database (`DriftPresenceDevices` in [drift_presence_stores.dart](../../../lib/features/signals/infrastructure/drift_presence_stores.dart)), storing `(peer_user_id, peer_device_id)`, P1/P2 ledger state, and `last_seen_ms` (written on offline transition / `gone` and at most once per minute while online; never synced or uploaded).
9. **D9 — Reciprocal per-phone switch ([presence_share_setting.dart](../../../lib/features/signals/application/presence_share_setting.dart)):**
   - Stored in `local_preferences` (`presence.share.v1`, `on` by default, missing/unreadable reads as `on`, per-phone). Off sends nothing (except one `gone` reason `2` on toggle off), clears all stored `last_seen_ms` (and receiving `gone` reason `2` clears that sender device's), hides all status and typing, yet still commits incoming frames so senders avoid dead keys.
10. **D10 — Disclosure revision 11 ([deployment_disclosure.dart](../../../lib/app/config/deployment_disclosure.dart)):**
    - Added `DisclosurePoint.onlineStatus(since: 11)` (`disclosureOnlineStatus`, en/fa) and moved `DeploymentDisclosure.distributed.revision` to `11`, triggering one-time re-acknowledgement for revision-10 users.
11. **D11 — UI surfaces ([peer_status_line.dart](../../../lib/features/signals/presentation/peer_status_line.dart), [online_status_settings_entry.dart](../../../lib/features/signals/presentation/online_status_settings_entry.dart)):**
    - Direct chat header (only when security gate is `ready`): typing (`"typing…"` / `"در حال نوشتن…"`, removing stale `"encrypted signal may lag"` caveat) > `"online"` > `"last seen today at …"` / `"last seen yesterday at …"` / `"last seen Mon, Oct 12"` (device local clock/time format) > nothing.
    - Group header: typing names (`"Alice is typing…"`, `"Alice and Bob are typing…"`, `"Alice and 2 others are typing…"`) > `"N members · M online"` (when M ≥ 1, verified active others only, groups ≤ 32) > member count.
    - Settings item 5c: `"Show my online status"` (`AppSwitchRow`, reads `presenceSharingProvider`, surfaces storage failure via snackbar while still applying off in-process).
    - Deleted leftover `watchPresence`, `applyPresence`, `PresenceProjection`, `PresenceMeaning`, and the 500-device map; kept `clearDisconnected`.

## Rationale

- Server [ADR-0022](../../../../docs/architecture/decisions/0022-the-gateway-holds-no-presence.md) stores no presence and drops undelivered `signal` frames; every dropped signal advances the sender's Double Ratchet chain and leaves a permanent skipped key on the receiver until the 2,000/session or 20,000/account repair bound.
- Unifying `CPVSV001` and `CPPSV001` behind one `SignalRouter` avoids double-opening frames, preserves compare-and-set ratchet serialization, and distinguishes volatile payloads from replayed durable envelopes without decoding.
- Bounding probes (P1/P2) and live frames (L1) in durable storage caps dead skipped keys to at most 8/day per pair without vanish (250 days to 2,000), 12 once for a device away indefinitely, and 800/day worst-case across 100 peer devices (25 days to 20,000), triggering lossless authenticated repair at the limit rather than losing messages.

## Costs

- Opening a shared chat reveals chat-open timing to verified peers (and 1024-byte signal timing to the server), disclosed in revision 11.
- Online status is a lower bound (omits unverified peers, non-watched periods, groups > 32, and P1-exhausted devices until they speak); vanished devices appear online up to 40 s; toggling the per-phone switch off also disables typing indicators.
- Adds schema 26 (`presence_devices`), disclosure revision 11, and up to 500 ms (later 1,500 ms in [ADR-101](101-presence-goodbye.md)) background socket-close delay.
